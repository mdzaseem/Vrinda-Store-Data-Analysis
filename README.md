# Vrinda Store Data Analysis using Excel

## About the Project

This is an Excel project I worked on to understand the sales performance of Vrinda Store in 2022.

While working on this project, I explored the sales data to find useful information about customers, orders, sales channels, and state-wise performance. I used Excel to organize the data and analyze different aspects of the business.

My main goal was to practice Excel on a business dataset and understand how data analysis can help answer real business questions.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel formulas
- Data analysis and reporting

## About the Dataset

The dataset contains order-level information, including:

- Order ID and Customer ID
- Gender and Age Group
- Order Date and Month
- Order Status
- Sales Channel
- Product Category, Size, and Quantity
- Sales Amount
- Shipping City and State
- B2B order information

The workbook also contains separate sheets for different sales summaries and analysis.

## What I Analyzed

During this project, I explored the following areas:

- Monthly Sales: Compared sales and order counts across the months of 2022.
- Order Status: Analyzed delivered, cancelled, returned, and refunded orders.
- Gender Analysis: Compared sales from men and women customers.
- Age Group Analysis: Explored customer age groups by gender.
- State-wise Sales: Identified the top five states by sales amount.
- Sales Channels: Compared order distribution across platforms such as Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, and Ajio.

## Some Findings from My Analysis

Here are a few findings from the workbook:

- Women accounted for a higher sales amount than men in the gender-wise sales summary.
- Maharashtra had the highest sales amount among the five states listed in the state-wise analysis.
- March had the highest monthly sales amount in the monthly summary.
- Amazon had the largest share of orders among the sales channels shown.
- The order-status summary contained 28,641 delivered orders, compared with 844 cancelled orders, 517 refunded orders, and 1,045 returned orders.
